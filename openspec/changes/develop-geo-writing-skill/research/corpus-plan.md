# GEO Writing Corpus Plan

## Purpose

Build a bounded, reviewable set of public examples that teaches the project
which editorial qualities generalize, which style choices are meaningfully
distinct, and which surface choices belong only to a publication platform.

The corpus is analysis evidence, not training data, a popularity leaderboard,
or a library of copied articles.

## Pilot size and balance

Start with at most sixty retained examples across five source families. Use a
larger discovery pool, but retain an example only when it adds a new structure,
style dimension, evidence pattern, or failure lesson.

Balance the retained set across:

- professional explainer;
- evidence-led authority article;
- problem-and-solution guide;
- brand or founder narrative;
- service-selection or comparison guide;
- answer-first FAQ or knowledge article.

Include several GEOEval-relevant industries such as local services, consumer
brands, professional services, software or digital products, and industrial or
business services. Do not infer that one successful style transfers unchanged
between regulated, high-consideration, and everyday consumer domains.

## Selection rules

Retain examples that are publicly accessible and strong in several of:

- clear audience and content purpose;
- useful, coherent information architecture;
- specific and traceable evidence;
- professional sentence and paragraph craft;
- recognizable but non-gimmicky voice;
- honest brand differentiation;
- originality and reader value;
- a conclusion appropriate to the article's purpose.

Exclude or separately label examples dominated by copied text, unverifiable
claims, clickbait, emotional manipulation, unsupported rankings, fabricated
authority, keyword stuffing, or platform rule violations. High engagement alone
is never an admission criterion.

## Evidence record

For each retained example, record:

- source URL, platform or publisher, author or organization, title, publication
  date when available, access date, language, industry, and rights or access
  note;
- intended audience, user question, content objective, and likely reader action;
- opening type, thesis, section outline, evidence types, claim strength,
  examples, transitions, conclusion, voice, sentence rhythm, and paragraph
  density;
- platform-only packaging such as title format, hashtags, emoji, account
  promotion, disclosure, and interaction conventions;
- two or three concise observations explaining why the example changes or
  confirms the style model;
- only the shortest excerpt necessary to support an observation, within source-
  specific quotation limits.

Do not store the complete body, bypass a login or paywall, or use automated
authenticated scraping.

## Analysis passes

1. **Within-platform:** identify recurrent structure and packaging without
   assuming popularity proves quality.
2. **Across-platform:** extract common editorial properties that survive channel
   changes.
3. **Within-style:** find the minimum traits that make each style recognizable.
4. **Across-industry:** identify rules that fail or need qualification by domain.
5. **Failure analysis:** record attractive but unusable patterns and the risk
   they create for factuality, trust, or GEO meaning.

## Exit condition

Close the pilot when:

- every retained style has multiple strong examples from more than one source
  family;
- reviewers can distinguish style traits from platform packaging;
- the quality rubric covers every repeated strength and reachable failure;
- adding ten more candidates produces no material new style dimension or changed
  action.

If those conditions are reached before sixty examples, stop. If they are not
reached, explain the missing coverage before expanding the corpus.
