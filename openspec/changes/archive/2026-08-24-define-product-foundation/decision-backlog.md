This is the single ledger for unresolved product-foundation decisions. It keeps
future questions durable without forcing the team to discuss them prematurely.
Approved decisions are reconciled into canonical product documents; rejected or
deferred choices remain here only when their rationale affects later work.

## How to use this backlog

- Discuss one decision series at a time and normally one root question per round.
- Use meeting records as input, not as automatic approval.
- Record a decision only after the product owner explicitly confirms it.
- Keep implementation mechanisms out until the required product behavior and
  boundary are stable.
- Mark each item `active`, `pending`, `decided`, `deferred`, or `rejected`.
