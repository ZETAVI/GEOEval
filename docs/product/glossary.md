# Product Glossary

Use one stable term for each product concept. Add a term only after its meaning is agreed; avoid importing implementation vocabulary into product discovery.

| Term | Working meaning | Must be distinguished from | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GEO | In this product, improving how a brand can be discovered and represented in generative-answer environments through evidence-based evaluation, guided promotional content, and real media publication without guaranteeing a model-level outcome | Traditional SEO, paid placement, generic content generation, or a guaranteed ranking service | Accepted |
| Evaluation | A structured assessment that preserves current platform-answer evidence and produces findings a user can interpret and act on; the initial product allows bounded repeated free evaluations under the current brand-information rule but does not provide continuous monitoring | An unexplained aggregate score, automated monitoring, or proof that publishing caused a later outcome | Accepted |
| Brand-directed question | A question that explicitly identifies the customer's business and is used to understand how an AI platform currently describes or characterizes it | An open question that tests whether the business appears without being forced into the question | Accepted |
| Open question | A question generated from the customer's profile to observe performance in a relevant discovery, recommendation, or need scenario without requiring the answer to focus on the customer | A brand-directed question | Accepted |
| AI recommendation index | One simple overall five-star rating over valid open-question samples, calculated as `5 × mention rate × (70% + 30% × average normalized position score)`; the customer sees one decimal and a half-star-rounded graphic, while the brand-directed question and positive or negative characteristics are excluded | A universal, permanent, scientifically precise, per-platform, or guaranteed measure of business quality or GEO performance | Accepted |
| Typical appearance position | The median raw relative position among valid open-question samples that mention the brand, shown as one approximate rank or the two middle ranks when they differ; it is a customer-readable description of where the brand usually appears and does not replace the average normalized position component of the AI recommendation index | An average rank, a rank calculated from the brand-directed question, a second score, or a guarantee of stable placement | Accepted |
| Sample parser | The per-answer interpretation role that uses shared evidence rules plus brand-directed or open-discovery task emphasis to extract the current brand, other explicit brands, meaningful relative positions, descriptions, associated characteristics, card interpretation, and exact evidence anchors across lists, tables, headings, paragraphs, and semantic structures without changing the original answer | Factual verification against the limited brand profile, simple ordinal matching, character position, cross-sample statistics or synthesis, a replacement answer, or the score formula itself | Accepted |
| Overall evaluation synthesizer | The cross-sample interpretation role that combines the brand context and all successful sample parses into the opening overall assessment, an evidence-sized set of no more than five broad positive and five broad negative themes, a practical reporting-level grouping of other-brand names, and the final optimization direction. It summarizes what the sampled AI platforms actually expressed rather than investigating or verifying real-world brand facts; it may group aliases, translations, store formats, and obvious subordinate brand lines from answer context under the consumer-recognizable parent brand while leaving an uncertain or clearly independent name separate. Program logic remains responsible for counts and scores, and retained evidence supports retry if synthesis fails | Per-sample fields, external brand research, factual verification, fragmented keyword matching, the recommendation-index formula, a master brand hierarchy, repeated platform sampling, or a replacement for original answer evidence | Accepted |
| Optimization direction | One evidence-grounded result of the overall evaluation with two coordinated representations: the customer sees no more than three concise cards covering problem, direction, evidence, and intended improvement, while a more comprehensive, objective, and explicit internal guidance is the only report-derived context passed to the promotional-article agent. The latest completed guidance remains usable after fine-grained profile edits or while a newer evaluation is incomplete, but current brand information and materials remain authoritative for facts | A guaranteed outcome, a customer-authored strategy, the complete report or raw samples passed to writing, a second independent AI conclusion, generic marketing advice, or the generated article itself | Accepted |
| Basic evaluation | One free assessment that shows the current observed problem and a concise optimization direction; the same unchanged evaluation-input revision may produce at most one successfully completed official evaluation, a valid zero-mention result counts, and an attempt that cannot produce a complete valid report after retries does not | A paid report, a failed sampling attempt, repeated official evaluations over unchanged inputs, or continuous monitoring | Accepted |
| Evaluation-input revision | The internally identifiable state of the normalized, evaluation-relevant brand information used to generate one non-editable four-question set. The unchanged revision cannot independently refresh that set and may produce one completed official evaluation, whose immutable snapshot preserves the exact brand fields, questions, and related context actually used after the current profile changes, without exposing a customer-facing version or rollback system | The one editable current brand profile, a customer-selected question variant, a provider or model configuration version, a complete history of every profile edit, or an article-input draft | Accepted |
| Current evaluation report | The brand's latest completed official report that remains current even when the editable brand profile later changes; it moves into evaluation history only when the customer starts a new official evaluation, while its own input and question snapshot never changes. Diagnosis emphasizes the current state and report, with prior reports available through a secondary read-only history rather than comparison or trend tooling | The current editable brand profile, an automatically invalidated report, a monitoring result, or a report rewritten from later information | Accepted |
| Valid sample | The one successful AI-platform answer and valid structured sample parse retained for one question on one platform; its complete answer is customer-visible, returned search sources remain internal, and provider or parser attempts do not become additional samples | A raw answer whose primary and fallback parsing both failed, a request attempt, retry count, source list shown to the customer, duplicated weighting, or guaranteed brand mention | Accepted |
| Evaluation platform set | The fixed initial set of DeepSeek, Doubao, Qwen, ERNIE Bot, and Tencent Hunyuan used for every evaluation and snapshotted with evaluation time in each report; each platform uses an internally selected explicit model representing its current mainstream public-facing experience | A customer-selected provider or model list, an implicit API model, a publishing-media platform list, or a configuration change that rewrites a historical report | Accepted |
| Brand profile | One independently managed business, brand, company, or storefront context containing one current, coherent set of required evaluation information and progressively completed optimization information, including its own flagship product or service category and optional materials; its evaluations, article work, orders, and publication results remain attached to it | A user account, a separate shared product catalog, a history of earlier profile versions, an agent-managed customer relationship, or one profile shared across businesses | Accepted |
| Industry catalog | The approved, versioned GEOEval-owned two-level classification whose stable primary and secondary identifiers, Chinese display names, boundaries, search aliases, recommendation subjects, and one `Other` fallback per primary help a non-expert customer express a coherent recommendation context | A copied local-life platform directory, government or statistical classification, licensed business scope, compliance system, product catalog, or a question template | Accepted |
| Industry selection | The one primary and dependent secondary industry chosen for the product or service that the current brand most wants customers to find and recommend it for; a selected `Other` also requires a concrete product-or-service phrase, and the complete selection participates in question generation and the evaluation-input snapshot | Every activity a mixed business performs, a legal or statistical principal-activity code, proof of regulatory eligibility, or the separately described flagship product or service category | Accepted |
| Current brand | The one brand profile currently selected as the common context for profile completion, diagnosis, evaluation, optimization, article generation, publishing orders, and publication management | Account identity or ownership of all brands by an agent | Accepted |
| Account role | The one terminal-customer, operations, administrator, or agent role assigned to an account in the initial product; the signed-in navigation, home, information, and authority follow that role, and the product provides no multi-role account or role switcher | Agent attribution on a terminal-customer account, a person using separate role-specific accounts, or permission to combine supporting-role authority | Accepted |
| Terminal-customer service home | The consumer-style **My brands** signed-in home, presented inside the platform's simple role-appropriate left-navigation shell. It supports an intentional no-brand entry and, once a current brand exists, a vertical service-page structure whose diagnosis, optimization, publishing, and media-resource regions change from polished capability introductions to concise current-brand results as real data becomes available; the same page also exposes lightweight brand-management entry | A management workbench, a dense dashboard, a forced end-to-end wizard, the public marketing homepage, a brand-only card list, or a duplicate of detailed report and order pages | Accepted |
| Archived brand | A previously used brand profile removed from ordinary current selection and new work while retaining its historical records and remaining restorable; it can be archived only when it has no active business work | A deleted unused profile, an archive folder, profile-version history, or a closed publishing order | Accepted |
| Operations user | An internal service fulfiller who exclusively claims one whole paid order at a time from the shared publishing pool, prepares publication variants, may use administrator-maintained media resources as non-blocking references, arranges actual publication, updates progress and exceptions, records accessible publication URLs and results, and initially handles customer-service contact when needed | A media-library maintainer, simultaneous co-owner of one order, system administrator, technical operator, or authority to change points, roles, or global business rules | Accepted |
| System administrator | A platform-governance role that manages accounts and access, authorized point adjustments, media-library facts and availability, packages, prices, global business rules, exceptional order intervention, and key operating records | The default role for article preparation, media publication, or routine order fulfilment | Accepted |
| Agent or reseller | An administrator-activated customer-acquisition and assisted-service role that cannot self-activate through public signup and may follow currently attributed customer progress and read complete current and historical customer-visible evaluation reports across that account's brands to explain results and services, while the customer retains data and commercial decisions; reattribution removes customer and report access but preserves the agent's own historical performance-order and commission records | The GEO promotional-article agent, a self-declared public account, the terminal customer, a separate agent relationship for each brand, access to internal prompts, traces, logs, sources or unrelated sensitive data, or authority to edit brand information, start or retry evaluation, spend points, confirm articles, submit paid orders, alter reports, or export customer reports | Accepted |
| Agent attribution | The account-level relationship created when a customer uses an approved agent acquisition entry without a visible invitation code, while direct public registration remains unattributed; all brands under the account share it, administrators may bind or correct it for new orders, agents cannot claim accounts, and history is never rewritten | Brand ownership, customer-visible commission, customer authorization to operate the account, or retroactive transfer of historical performance | Accepted |
| Agent customer portfolio | The agent's customer-management hierarchy rooted in currently attributed terminal-customer accounts: each customer opens its own brand list, and each brand owns the relevant evaluation, optimization, and publishing records; performance orders, commission, and withdrawals remain separate agent-level modules connected by links and immutable business ownership rather than duplicated customer-page ledgers | A flat feed mixing customers, brands, reports, orders, and commission; a second owner of customer records; a brand-level attribution model; or the agent's performance and withdrawal ledger | Accepted |
| Publishing pool | The shared operations view in which whole paid publishing orders wait for one eligible operations user to claim exclusive fulfilment responsibility; unstarted work can be returned with a reason, while started work requires administrator reassignment | The customer's order list, a media inventory, splitting one order among several first-release owners, or simultaneous ownership of one order by every operations user | Accepted |
| Publication work item | One internal operations task representing one purchased publication quantity unit, using pending, publishing, published, or exception; an operator may use an existing media-resource candidate or an unlisted account, and one recorded accessible publication URL plus the required result facts can complete the item without a catalog match or automated URL-to-platform check; one item can produce at most one valid publication result | The whole order's aggregate state, an operations owner, a retry attempt, an article variant selection, a required media-resource selection, or more than one completed-publication count | Accepted |
| Basic brand information | The subset required before a brand can enter diagnosis: one approved industry selection with a concrete product-or-service phrase when its secondary category is `Other`, two brand characteristics, company or store name, region, contact person, and mobile number; terminal-customer registration may collect it for the first brand or let the customer skip and complete it later, while every later brand requires the same information before diagnosis | The more complete information required before article generation, every activity a mixed business performs, a guarantee that every registered account already has a brand, or sufficient information for article generation | Accepted |
| Article information | The progressively completed information within the current brand profile that is required before generating a core promotional article, including a flagship product or service category expressed through its name, main characteristic description, price range, and suitable audiences; the article-generation page updates this same information rather than maintaining a task-specific copy | The generated core article itself, a separate product catalog, or a separate set of inputs for each generation | Accepted |
| Prepared material digest | The structured Markdown writing context produced once from a brand's optional PDFs, images, and text materials by extracting and organizing usable content, interpreting relevant visuals, retaining source references, and marking uncertain, conflicting, or unreadable content without overwriting structured brand fields; it is attached to the brand and reused until source material is deleted or replaced | The original file, a result requiring separate customer confirmation, an unverified factual claim, a customer-facing article, a replacement brand profile, or a generic knowledge base | Accepted |
| GEO optimization and publishing service | The lightweight and fast paid action layer that turns the customer's current multi-platform problems and evaluation context into guidance, a customer-confirmed core article, differently optimized variants, managed media publication through company resources, and visible delivery results | A standalone article sale, an isolated media storefront, free evaluation, or guaranteed improvement in AI mentions or ranking | Accepted |
| Core promotional article | The one GEO-oriented, customer-editable title and complete body generated from the brand profile and evaluation context that establishes the main subject matter for later variants and publication; the system assigns an available writer Skill without a customer-facing style choice, and each brand has one current unsubmitted article until paid order submission fixes it as the order basis | Title candidates, a summary or keyword bundle, platform-specific variants, a collection of candidate histories, a brand-profile field, or a promise that every published version will use identical wording | Accepted |
| Publication variant | One of multiple differently angled and polished versions produced from the confirmed core article for operations to publish directly or adjust further while maintaining its main subject matter | A separately purchased or separately customer-confirmed core article | Accepted |
| Media library | The administrator-maintained live Media Supply catalog exposed through a standalone professional customer entry and maintained through a separate administrator experience; one media platform is one first-release priced sales unit, defaults to disabled, and becomes customer-buyable only when enabled with a whole-point single-publication price, while up to fifty approved full or masked concrete-resource examples remain non-selectable supporting context and operations references rather than fulfilment prerequisites; internal procurement cost, source/contact data, notes, paid-order snapshots, and publication results retain separate ownership and lifecycle | A separate one-to-one sales Listing, one shared page for customer browsing and administrator maintenance, an operations-owned order queue, a required inventory allocation system, a customer-priced specific account, unsupported influence or effect claims, unpaid price reservation, a change that rewrites historical orders, or previously delivered publication links | Accepted |
| Random publishing package | A publishing choice with a defined successful-publication quantity and total point price in which the platform assigns eligible media and may replace unavailable placements within the stated scope; the customer can inspect the library but cannot select or require particular destinations | Precise publishing, a quantity of identical articles, or a guarantee of particular media destinations | Accepted |
| Precise publishing | A publishing choice in which the customer selects platforms and quantities from the explicitly priced media library and pays the summed point price; selections cannot be silently substituted, and customer service handles an impossible placement by agreeing a replacement with the customer or arranging an administrator point return | A randomly allocated publishing package or a customer self-service refund flow | Accepted |
| Point | The whole-number, unified customer-visible account balance shared across that terminal user's brands and used to purchase publishing services, with a fixed conversion of ten points per renminbi and obtainable only through customer recharge or administrator allocation; internal records distinguish customer-funded and granted origins without showing separate balances or uses to the customer | A decimal or brand-specific balance, user-selectable balance type, reward, status level, transferable currency, or guaranteed cash-equivalent refund | Accepted |
| Recharge order | A customer-created self-service online-payment order for a shortcut or custom whole-renminbi amount that credits points only after confirmed success and is shown as pending payment, confirming, recharge successful, or closed | An administrator point grant, publishing order, cash-equivalent point balance, automatic cash refund, or a failed payment that credited points | Accepted |
| Point change | One append-only increase or decrease in the unified customer balance, showing the customer its time, amount, resulting balance, simple reason, and related business record while keeping funded-versus-granted composition and internal handling detail private | An editable balance-history row, a separate customer-visible funded or granted balance, or an agent commission record | Accepted |
| Agent commission | A renminbi-denominated rebate calculated as final commission-eligible customer-funded points divided by ten and multiplied by the fixed agent rate captured on the order; it is pending after payment, becomes effective for successfully delivered and non-returned consumption when the order completes or closes, is rounded to fen, and is accompanied by a source-aware order detail | Commission on recharge alone, platform-granted or returned points, customer points, an immediate cash entitlement at order submission, or a retroactively changed rate | Accepted |
| Payout profile | An agent's reusable, sensitive set of domestic bank-transfer instructions for an individual or enterprise recipient, containing only core account data plus routing fields conditionally required by the company's bank process; each withdrawal stores a snapshot | Tax identity, an opening-bank street address, a public agent profile, or authorization to withdraw without review | Accepted |
| Withdrawal request | An agent-initiated request to pay part of available commission through administrator review and offline company transfer, temporarily freezing the requested amount until completion, rejection, or recorded payment failure | Customer point withdrawal, automatic payout, an order refund, or commission becoming effective | Accepted |
| Customer invoice profile | Reusable purchaser information for the one electronic ordinary invoice available to a successful renminbi recharge order: individual name and receiving email, or enterprise name, unified social credit code or taxpayer identification number, and receiving email; recharge identity and actual paid amount come from the order | A special VAT invoice profile, address, telephone, bank details, personal identity number, agent payout account, publishing-order invoice request, point record, or agent tax information | Accepted |
| Recharge invoice request | The one electronic ordinary-invoice request attached to a successful renminbi recharge order, locked after submission and shown as processing, needs correction, or issued; operations creates and emails the invoice outside the product, then confirms the uploaded result | A system-sent invoice email, tax-platform integration, customer self-service cancellation, red invoicing, reissue, or an invoice for point consumption | Accepted |
| Publishing order | The paid customer agreement that fixes one confirmed core article, one random package or set of precise selections, required points, progressive publication results, and a simple customer-visible state of pending handling, publishing, exception handling, completed, or closed; its customer page leads with status and quantity progress, preserves the purchased commitment, and supports viewing and contacting service without self-service cancellation, refund, media change, or acceptance | An individual publication result, internal operations task, an editable unpaid selection, or proof of an AI-platform outcome | Accepted |
| Publication result | One successful-publication record containing the published title, accessible URL, publication time, and status, with an optional media-resource reference when an existing catalog candidate was used; the accessible URL and required result facts—not the optional catalog match—drive completion, while the customer card omits internally retained account/channel detail, optional proof, internal note, and correction history | A manually selected article variant, a mandatory media-resource link, automated proof of URL-to-platform identity, proof that publication caused an AI-platform improvement, or a promise that a third-party link remains available permanently | Accepted |
| Notification | A role-targeted in-product notice with a short result, time, read state, and related-business entry, retained through a notification center and optionally surfaced to an online user through a browser-tab indicator or top-right prompt without refresh | The underlying business event, an internal log, a direct execution surface for consequential actions, or a guarantee of SMS or email delivery | Accepted |
| User-facing error message | A concise, role-appropriate explanation of what happened, what the user's action or business result is affected by, and what the user can do next | A provider error, retry trace, stack trace, internal log entry, or generic “operation failed” text without an action | Accepted |
| Finding | An evidence-backed observation produced by an evaluation | Recommendation, prediction, or implementation task | Accepted |
| Recommendation | A proposed action linked to one or more findings and expected outcomes | Finding or guaranteed result | Accepted |

These terms were accepted through the confirmed product-foundation change on 2026-08-24. Later work may refine a term only through an explicit product decision that updates this current owner.
