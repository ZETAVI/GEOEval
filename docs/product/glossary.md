# Product Glossary

Use one stable term for each product concept. Add a term only after its meaning is agreed; avoid importing implementation vocabulary into product discovery.

| Term | Working meaning | Must be distinguished from | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GEO | Optimization of how an entity is discovered, represented, or cited in generative-answer environments; the project's exact scope remains to be agreed | Traditional SEO, paid placement, generic content generation | Provisional |
| Evaluation | A repeatable assessment that produces evidence a user can interpret and act on | An unexplained aggregate score | Provisional |
| Finding | An evidence-backed observation produced by an evaluation | Recommendation, prediction, or implementation task | Provisional |
| Recommendation | A proposed action linked to one or more findings and expected outcomes | Finding or guaranteed result | Provisional |

Terms become accepted only when the active product-definition change is approved and reconciled.
