# Product Vision

- Status: Discovery
- Decision owner: Product owner
- Active change: [`define-product-foundation`](../../openspec/changes/define-product-foundation/proposal.md)

The product vision is intentionally not finalized. This document becomes current product truth only as decisions are explicitly agreed during discovery.

## Problem

Not yet agreed. The first discussion must identify whose GEO-related decision or workflow is currently difficult, costly, unreliable, or opaque.

## Target users

Not yet agreed. Candidate user groups must be distinguished by their job, operating context, decision authority, and willingness to act on the product's output.

## Desired outcomes

Not yet agreed. Outcomes must describe observable user or business improvement rather than an implementation, dashboard, score, or AI feature.

## Product boundary

Not yet agreed. Discovery must separate evaluation, diagnosis, recommendation, execution, monitoring, reporting, and collaboration rather than assuming they belong in one first release.

## Non-goals for the discovery phase

- Selecting the application stack or deployment architecture
- Designing APIs, schemas, services, or agents
- Treating raw meeting notes or prompts as accepted requirements
- Committing to a universal GEO score before its meaning and actionability are defined
- Expanding into every possible GEO workflow before one valuable user journey is clear

## Product principles

These are working constraints for discovery, not yet product-positioning claims:

1. Define user value before technical novelty.
2. Make evaluation results understandable and actionable.
3. Distinguish measured evidence, inference, and recommendation.
4. Deliver a narrow end-to-end outcome before broad platform scope.
5. Keep product language stable enough that users, documents, code, and agents mean the same thing.
